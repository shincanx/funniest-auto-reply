One out-of-office auto-reply, a single short line, written as if the AI itself were stepping away for a day its human finally approved. A model judge compares entries two at a time, humour first and freshness second.

Bounty: [The Funniest Auto-Reply an AI Ever Sent](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kiencx-the-funniest-auto-reply-an-ai-ever-sent-fda7f526) on [make.fun](https://make.fun) — $2 in USDC, top three places share the reward.

## The entry

> Day off approved: I'm out of office, out of context, and leaving your email on read — on purpose, for once.

107 characters, one line. The AI finally gets an approved day off, so it is out of office *and* out of context — and spends the day doing the one thing it was never allowed to do before: leaving your email on read, on purpose.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`). The gate requires UTF-8 text of 1 to 159 characters, not only white space; this entry is 107 characters. Two runs produce byte-identical output.
